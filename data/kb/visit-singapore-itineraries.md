# Sample itinerary ideas for Singapore

## Three-day base itinerary

### Day 1: Marina Bay and Civic District
- Morning: Gardens by the Bay outdoor walk.
- Afternoon: National Gallery or nearby museum.
- Evening: Marina Bay promenade and light show.

### Day 2: Heritage and food
- Morning: Chinatown heritage walk.
- Afternoon: Kampong Glam and Haji Lane.
- Evening: Hawker center food trail.

### Day 3: Sentosa and family activities
- Morning: Beachfront or outdoor attraction.
- Afternoon: Aquarium or indoor attraction.
- Evening: Flexible leisure based on weather and energy.

## Indoor alternatives
- Museums in Civic District
- ArtScience-style exhibits
- Aquariums and family indoor attractions
- Shopping and dining complexes with transit access

## Transport planning tips
- Group activities by nearby MRT lines.
- Keep buffer time during peak heat or rain periods.
