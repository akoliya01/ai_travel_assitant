# Singapore destination notes

Singapore is a compact city-state with many districts that are easy to combine in short trips.

## Districts and neighbourhoods
- Marina Bay: skyline views, waterfront walks, gardens, and major attractions.
- Civic District: museums, historical landmarks, and arts venues.
- Chinatown and Kampong Glam: cultural streets, heritage architecture, and local food.
- Little India: temples, markets, and vibrant street life.
- Sentosa: beaches, family attractions, and resorts.

## Transportation
- MRT is the primary way for visitors to move around quickly.
- Public buses complement MRT for short transfers.
- Contactless cards and mobile payments are widely accepted.
- Taxis and ride-hailing are available but usually cost more than transit.

## Practical guidance
- Heat and humidity are common year-round; hydration and light clothing are useful.
- Sudden rain showers are common; carry a compact umbrella.
- Many attractions are close to stations, so planning by neighbourhood reduces travel time.
