# Essential travel information for Singapore

## Culture and etiquette
- Dress modestly in religious sites and follow site-specific signs.
- Queueing culture is common in transport and food locations.
- English is widely used, which helps independent travelers.

## Food and experiences
- Hawker centres are popular for affordable local meals.
- Typical food exploration areas include Maxwell, Lau Pa Sat, and neighbourhood hawker hubs.
- Night walks along Marina Bay and river areas are common visitor activities.

## Family-friendly planning
- Mix interactive attractions with open parks.
- Include weather-safe indoor alternatives such as museums, aquariums, and science centers.
